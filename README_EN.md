## About Us
Molix is a technical team focused on molecular AI, dedicated to applying AI technology to the early stages of drug discovery, materials design, and chemical R&D.

We provide four core services for drug, materials, and chemical R&D teams: molecular generation, model training, molecular screening, and property prediction, covering the key steps from candidate molecule design to developability assessment.

**Core Value:** By replacing part of early-stage experimental screening with AI prediction, we help clients shorten R&D cycles, reduce trial-and-error costs, and focus R&D resources on high-potential molecules.

### 🧠 Model Training
- Integrate molecular data and AI algorithms to build prediction models for specific tasks.
- Cover key developability metrics such as target activity, physicochemical properties, metabolic stability, and safety, supporting customized training based on client data.

### ⚗️ Molecular Generation
- Based on generative AI models, inversely generate candidate molecules according to target properties or structural constraints.
- Support multi-dimensional constraints such as molecular scaffolds, functional groups, molecular weight, and LogP. Generated results can directly enter the property prediction and screening stages.

### 🔍 Molecular Screening
- Support rapid virtual screening of large-scale molecular libraries, with simultaneous multi-property evaluation.
- Output prioritized candidate molecule lists, supporting subsequent experimental validation and optimization.

### 📊 ADMET Prediction

Cover the five major ADMET dimensions: absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, and toxicity.
Provide ADMET property prediction and optimization suggestions, supporting developability assessment decisions.

- **Absorption (A)**: Caco-2 permeability, aqueous solubility, P-gp substrate

- **Distribution (D)**: Plasma protein binding rate, blood-brain barrier penetration

- **Metabolism (M)**: CYP450 enzyme inhibition, liver microsomal stability

- **Excretion (E)**: Clearance, half-life prediction

- **Toxicity (T)**: hERG cardiotoxicity, Ames mutagenicity, hepatotoxicity
